What if salvation is not only something to believe, but a life to enter?

The *Gospel of Philip* is one of the most intriguing writings preserved among the ancient texts discovered at Nag Hammadi. Rather than telling the story of Jesus, it speaks through fragments and images—bread and water, oil and fire, pearls and light, resurrection and the mysterious bridal chamber. Beneath these images runs a persistent invitation: do not merely understand the truth. Participate in it.

This modern devotional gathers the *Gospel of Philip* into thirty contemplative readings, each beginning with an ancient passage and opening it into a meditation for contemporary life. Along the way, Philip's strange and beautiful imagery becomes a doorway into questions of identity, desire, healing, self-knowledge, transformation, divine love, and the possibility of resurrection beginning before death.

These pages are not intended to explain away Philip's mysteries. They invite you to sit with them.

Whether you come as a Christian, a contemplative, a seeker, or simply someone curious about the forgotten diversity of early Christian spirituality, these readings offer an opportunity to slow down and encounter an ancient voice with fresh attention.

Receive the bread. Enter the water. Put on the light.

And discover what it might mean to become what you behold.
